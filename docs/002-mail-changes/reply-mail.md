## 2. Decisions I've made — happy to change any of these

*Roughly least to most confident, so the ones I'd most like a second opinion on are first.*

**Video vintage** — worth knowing: the sports-psychology videos are from Swim England's 2020 lockdown series (they talk about training at home and "unprecedented times"). I went looking for current replacements: for **nutrition** there's a much fresher option, so the library now leads with Swim England's 2026 nutrition Q&A (real swimmers' questions — race-day meals, pre-session snacks, electrolytes) alongside the original essentials video. For **psychology** the 2020 series is still the newest thing Swim England has on race-day techniques — their recent mental-side videos ([Stronger Without the Scales](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oc61NFyB5W8), [fuelling up and feeling great](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=13fahAfup3c)) are about body image and energy rather than competition nerves, so I've kept the emotions wheel and 5-4-3-2-1 grounding videos — the techniques themselves don't date. Happy to add the newer wellbeing ones too if you like the look of them.

**Warm-up video & stretches** — the video library has dedicated **Warm-Up** and **Stretches** sections, filled with picks from the same Swim England playlist your links came from (the supple strength warm-up, mobility for starts & turns, streamlining for young swimmers, and the arms/shoulders, hamstrings and splits flexibility sessions). Shout if you'd have picked different ones.

**Logo treatment** — after trying a few variations ([you can see them all here](https://michal-ciechan.github.io/torquay-leander/samples/logo/) — tap any one to preview it on the real site, then hit **Share** to send your choice to me) I've gone with adding a **thick white outline around the words**, to make it more readable at the smaller size a logo actually appears at. The standard logo looks fine small on the black footer, so we could keep that one there, but for now I've used the same thick outline in both places for consistency.

**Hero banner** — I've kept the full-screen banner for now but taken the "Building Confidence…" headline right down — it now sits only slightly larger than the intro text beneath it, the same on every device, so the photo carries the impact rather than the type. The original large-type version is preserved as a sample for comparison. I also mocked up four treatments — full screen, compact (~3/4 height), a half-height banner, and a side-by-side layout — and scored them against a fixed set of criteria; [the comparison and scorecard are here](https://michal-ciechan.github.io/torquay-leander/samples/hero/), with real above-the-fold screenshots under each option for iPhone SE, iPhone 15 Pro Max, iPad, and 720p/1080p screens. Compact actually scored best (it shows visitors the page continues below), so say the word if you'd like to switch to it.

**Championships programme design** — the 2026 programme is now shown as a **timeline** — one date node per day, with the two Sunday sessions side by side. [The other treatments I mocked up are here](https://michal-ciechan.github.io/torquay-leander/samples/club-championships/) — session cards (the previous look), calendar-style date tiles, and a colour-coded stroke × distance grid that answers "when is my event?" at a glance — plus full vs short event naming. Happy to switch to any of them.

**Contact form colour** — I've lightened the form to a white card with light grey fields, which is a big improvement on the dark version. If you'd like a different look, [here are four treatments side by side](https://michal-ciechan.github.io/torquay-leander/samples/contact/) — just tell me which you prefer.

**Where the enquiry split lives** — the Contact page now has **Membership** and **General enquiry** tabs. On the home page I've kept only the membership form, since that's what most first-time visitors are there for, with a small "not about joining?" link through to the general form. Happy to show both on the home page if you'd rather.

**Contact page** — rather than only fixing the form on the home page, I've given it its own **Contact** page in the menu, so people don't have to scroll to the bottom of the home page to find it: https://michal-ciechan.github.io/torquay-leander/contact/

**"FINA approved"** — worth flagging that FINA renamed itself to World Aquatics a couple of years ago, so the term is slightly dated. I've kept FINA since it's what the club and most suppliers still say, but easy to swap if you'd rather be current.
